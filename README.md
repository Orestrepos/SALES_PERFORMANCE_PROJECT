
# Sales Performance Dashboard | Business Intelligence Project
# Análisis Comercial – Business Intelligence

## Business problem
Companies often have sales data but lack of clear visibility to understand performance, identify opportunities, and make informed decisions.
This project simulates a retail business scenario where sales data is analyzed to improve decision-making.
 
## Solution
I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables:
- Sales performance tracking over time
- Customer segmentation based on behavior
- Identification of top and underperforming products
- Analysis by region, category and customer group

## Key Features 
- KPI tracking (total sales, average ticket, sales by category)
- Sales trend analysis
- Customer clustering (behavior-based segmentation)
- Dynamic filtering (region, category, customer segment)

## Key insights
- Sales experienced a decline in 2018 followed by a strong recovery starting 2019.
- Customer segmentation revealed three distinct groups with different purchasing behaviors:
  - Low-ticket, high frequency customers
  - High-value customers (premium)
  - Medium-value, stable costumers
- The Bikes category consistently drives the highest sales performance. 

## Business Impact
This dashboard allow businesses to:
- Identify growth opportunities
- Improve customer targeting strategies
- Monitor performance in real time
- Support data-driven commercial decisions
